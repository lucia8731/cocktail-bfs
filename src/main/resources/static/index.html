THE ENGINEERING NOTE

Small dataset. Real decisions.

At 24 recipes, a linear scan would be simpler. These structures are here to make their behavior inspectable and their trade-offs explicit — not to claim a benchmark win. The graph is built once in O(N² × I) expected time; path queries then run in O(V + E). The optional queue snapshots add O(V²) time and space in the worst case. Metrics count actual operations, not invented latency.
